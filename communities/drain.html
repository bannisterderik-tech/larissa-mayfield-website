About Drain

Living in
Drain.

Drain is a small timber town in Douglas County along Highway 99. It offers very affordable large-acreage properties, rolling hills, and a genuine rural lifestyle that draws buyers seeking self-sufficiency.

  • — Affordable large acreage
  • — Timber & ranch properties
  • — Douglas County schools
  • — 40 min to Roseburg
  • — Off-grid potential
